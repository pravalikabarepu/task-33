NETFLIX DATA ANALYSIS

Project Overview
This project analyzes the netflix content

Dataset
dataset: Netflix Titles dataset
Number of records: 8807
Main columns used:
- show_id
- type
- Titles
- director
- cast
- country
- date_added
- release_year
- rating
- duration
- listed_in
- description

Analysis Performed
1. Movies vs TV Shows
2. Netflix content releases by year
3. Top 10 genres
4. Genre trends over time
5. Content rating distribution
6. Country-wise content Analysis
7. Average movie duration over time
8. TV seasons trends
9. Additional analysis- movie vs TV shows by year
10. key statistical summaries
11. Automatically identify some key findings

Key Insights
1. Movies make up a larger share of the Netflix catalog than TV shows.
2. Netflix content additions increased significantly over time, with strong growth during the 2010s.
3. International movies, dramas, and comedies are among the most common genres in the catalog.
4. Genre popularity changed over time, showing different patterns of growth and decline.
5. TV-MA and TV-14 ate the most common content rating in the dataset.
6. The Netflix catalog contains content from many countries, showing its international reach(United States).
7. The average duration for the movie is 200 minutes in the year(1960-1970).
8. The averge Tv shows for the year (200-2005) has the highest-9 seasons.
9. Movies has the highest releases in the year(2016-2018). TV shows has the highest releases in the year 2020.

Conclusion
The analysis provides an overview of Netflix's content composition, release patterns, genre distribution, and audience ratings using 
data-driven visulaizations.
